Give the student the textbook (RAG)

Gemini is a smart student in an exam with no school handbook. RAG puts the handbook on the desk.

1. User asks: “How do I get a transcript?”
2. Your code splits the handbook into chunks (pages / headings).
3. Find chunks that share words with the question (later: meaning search).
4. Send those chunks + the question to the model.
5. Model answers. If the text is missing, it must say it cannot find it.
