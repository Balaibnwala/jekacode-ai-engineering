Latency = how long you wait

Latency is the time from clicking Send to seeing the full answer. We measure it in ms (1000 ms = 1 second).

Gemini Flash — often a short wait
Grok mini — often similar; depends on network
DeepSeek — can be a bit longer from some African ISPs
Ollama on a school laptop — your computer is the kitchen
